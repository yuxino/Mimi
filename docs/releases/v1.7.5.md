## English

- Fixed a jump in live bilingual subtitles on desktop. A new short line takes only its own height, and a long line shows its newest text from the first frame.
- “Translating” appears when you hover over the subtitle window or reach its controls with the keyboard. Service errors remain visible. When you scroll back, “Back to live” stays below the captions and accepts the first click.
- On macOS, hovering over the captions reveals the language button and toolbar together. After returning to live captions, you can move straight onto the language button and open it with one click. Closing the panel and moving away hides the controls again.
- On macOS, clicking subtitle text keeps the window in place. Dragging moves the window from the point you pressed.
- Corrected the German “All applications” option in the audio source picker.

Thanks to @windowsair for reporting the subtitle jump and contributing a recording and frame-by-frame comparison in [#397](https://github.com/yuxino/Mimi/issues/397).

Update from Mimi or download the installer for your platform. This release also includes the signed Android APK. We checked scrolling with repeatable offline captions and replayed subtitles returned by Doubao for two film excerpts.

## 中文

- 修复桌面端实时双语字幕上下跳动的问题。新到的短句只占实际行高，长句从第一帧就能看到末尾。
- 鼠标移入字幕窗口或用键盘聚焦控件时才显示「翻译中」。服务错误仍然可见。回看字幕时，「回到实时」固定在字幕下方，点一次即可回到最新一句。
- 在 macOS 上，鼠标移入字幕正文，左上的语言栏和右侧工具栏会一起出现。点过「回到实时」后，直接移到语言栏就能点开面板。再次收起面板、移开鼠标，两侧控件会一起隐藏。
- 在 macOS 上单击字幕正文时，窗口保持原位。按住拖动时，窗口会跟随最初按下的位置移动。
- 修正音源选择器中「全部应用」的德语文案。

感谢 @windowsair 在 [#397](https://github.com/yuxino/Mimi/issues/397) 报告字幕跳动，并提供录屏与逐帧对照。

可在 Mimi 内更新，也可下载对应平台的安装包。本版同时提供签名的 Android APK。我们用离线字幕反复检查滚动，并按原时间重放了豆包为两段电影对白生成的字幕。
