## English

- Fixed a jump in live bilingual subtitles on desktop. Showing or clearing “Translating” keeps the reading area in place. A new short line takes only its own height, and a long line shows its newest text from the first frame.
- On macOS, clicking subtitle text keeps the window in place. Dragging moves the window from the point you pressed.
- Corrected the German “All applications” option in the audio source picker.

Thanks to @windowsair for reporting the subtitle jump and contributing a recording and frame-by-frame comparison in [#397](https://github.com/yuxino/Mimi/issues/397).

Update from Mimi or download the installer for your platform. This release also includes the signed Android APK. The scrolling checks use offline captions.

## 中文

- 修复桌面端实时双语字幕上下跳动的问题。「翻译中」出现或消失时，阅读区保持原位。新到的短句只占实际行高，长句从第一帧就能看到末尾。
- 在 macOS 上单击字幕正文时，窗口保持原位。按住拖动时，窗口会跟随最初按下的位置移动。
- 修正音源选择器中「全部应用」的德语文案。

感谢 @windowsair 在 [#397](https://github.com/yuxino/Mimi/issues/397) 报告字幕跳动，并提供录屏与逐帧对照。

可在 Mimi 内更新，也可下载对应平台的安装包。本版同时提供签名的 Android APK。滚动验证使用了离线字幕。
