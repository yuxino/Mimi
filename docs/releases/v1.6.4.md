## English

- Kept current subtitles stable when earlier speech or translation results arrive late.
- Improved recovery after interrupted reconnections and Android audio backpressure.
- Fixed interrupted window resizing, stale settings previews, and rapid name or font changes.
- Reduced repeated subtitle rendering and unnecessary background work.

Update from Settings → Software Update, or download the installer below. Portable Windows users can replace their existing executable after quitting Mimi.

Contributor: @yuxino.

## 中文

- 防止较早的识别或翻译结果迟到时干扰当前字幕。
- 改善重连中断后的恢复，以及 Android 音频积压后的恢复。
- 修复窗口缩放被打断、设置预览过期，以及快速修改名称或字体时的保存问题。
- 减少重复的字幕渲染和不必要的后台工作。

可在「设置 → 软件更新」中更新，也可下载下方安装包。Windows 便携版请先退出 Mimi，再替换原有程序。

贡献者：@yuxino。
