## English

- Added Russian as an interface language on desktop and Android, with Russian setup and usage guides.
- Simplified the Android floating captions. Use the top-right switch to move or resize the panel, or lock it to interact with the app underneath.
- Improved original and translated caption continuity when switching videos with OpenAI and Gemini. Fixed lost final captions when stopping OpenAI translation.
- Improved Baidu caption stability and recovery after silence.

OpenAI may still omit original text or misrecognize speech and music. Same-language speech follows the selected service's translation behavior.

Thanks @KiepGaCon for suggesting the Android caption changes.

## 中文

- 桌面端和 Android 新增俄语界面，并提供俄语配置和使用指南。
- 简化 Android 字幕浮窗。用右上角开关启用移动和缩放，或锁定浮窗，直接操作下方应用。
- 改善使用 OpenAI 和 Gemini 切换视频时原文与译文的连续性，修复停止 OpenAI 翻译时可能丢失尾句的问题。
- 改善百度字幕的稳定性及静音后的恢复。

OpenAI 仍可能缺失原文，或无法准确识别语音和音乐。同语种语音按所选服务的翻译行为处理。

感谢 @KiepGaCon 提出 Android 字幕浮窗改进建议。
