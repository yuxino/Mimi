## English

- Improved original and translated caption continuity when switching videos with OpenAI and Gemini.
- Fixed lost final captions when stopping OpenAI translation.
- Improved Baidu caption stability and recovery after silence.

OpenAI can still omit original text or misrecognize speech and music. Automatically skipping translation for same-language speech is not included in this update.

## 中文

- 改善使用 OpenAI 和 Gemini 切换视频时，原文与译文的连续性。
- 修复停止 OpenAI 翻译时可能丢失尾句的问题。
- 改善百度字幕的稳定性及静音后的恢复。

OpenAI 仍可能缺失原文，或无法准确识别语音和音乐。本次更新尚未实现自动跳过同语种翻译。
