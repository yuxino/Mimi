## English

- Hover over the desktop subtitles to see “Translating” and one timing together beside the tools. The language selector stays on one line, and only the button under the pointer is highlighted. Narrow windows place the readout below the buttons. Open the language panel for the individual API and translation timings.
- OpenAI Realtime and other streaming services now show translation activity as unfinished text arrives. Finishing the translation clears the label while the last measured timing stays visible on hover.
- Quickly dragging the subtitle scrollbar back to the bottom resumes live captions even when the last scroll arrives after you release the mouse.
- On Android, start or stop captions from Quick Settings. At the top of More, you can also turn on a floating Mimi logo that tucks against the screen edge after three seconds, or keep a notification handy to start captions. Each has its own switch. You can hide the logo while captions are running.
- When locking the phone interrupts system-audio captions, tap **Continue captions** after unlocking and confirm Android's sharing prompt to return to the video. This reminder requires notifications and also works with the persistent shortcut turned off. Thanks to @Excarius for suggesting the shortcuts and explaining the lock-screen interruption in [#401](https://github.com/yuxino/Mimi/issues/401).

Update in Mimi or download the installer for your platform. This release also includes the signed Android APK.

## 中文

- 鼠标移入桌面字幕窗，「翻译中」和一个延迟读数并排显示在工具按钮旁。语言切换收为单行，指向哪个按钮，哪个按钮才高亮。窗口较窄时，读数移到按钮下方。展开语言面板可分别查看已有的接口和翻译耗时。
- OpenAI Realtime 等实时服务逐步返回译文时，也能及时显示「翻译中」。译文完成后提示消失，鼠标留在字幕窗内仍能看到最近的延迟读数。
- 回看字幕时快速把滚动条拖到底再松手，即使最后一段滚动稍晚到达，下一句也会继续跟上。
- Android 现在可以从快捷设置启停字幕。「更多」顶部可以分别打开屏边悬浮球和常驻通知。悬浮球沿用 Mimi 的应用图标，可拖动，闲置三秒自动贴边。字幕运行时也可以单独把球收起来。
- 如果锁屏中断了系统声音字幕，解锁后点「继续字幕」，确认 Android 的共享提示即可回到视频。允许系统通知就能收到这条提醒，常驻入口可以关着。感谢 @Excarius 在 [#401](https://github.com/yuxino/Mimi/issues/401) 中提出这些入口，并说明锁屏时遇到的问题。

可在 Mimi 内更新，也可下载对应平台的安装包。本版同时提供签名的 Android APK。
