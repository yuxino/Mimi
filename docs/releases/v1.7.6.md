## English

- **Start Android captions without leaving your video.** In **More → Caption shortcuts**, add **Mimi captions** to Quick Settings. You can also enable a floating shortcut or a notification that offers Start while captions are off. Each has its own switch, and you can turn either off during a session. The running notification offers Stop. On Android 13 and later, fresh installs can ask once for notifications when you start from inside Mimi. Starting from a shortcut skips that optional prompt.
- Drag Mimi to the left or right edge of the screen. Tap the character, then tap **Start** or **Stop** beside it. The text follows your system font size. The button stays open while pressed and disappears after three idle seconds. Mimi stays at the edge. Stopping captions ends capture.
- If locking the phone interrupts a **system-audio-only** session, allow notifications to receive **Continue captions** after unlocking. Tap it and confirm Android’s sharing prompt to return to the video. The reminder works with the idle notification switched off. Sessions using the microphone stay stopped. This release uses Android’s standard consent flow on every new system-audio session, including on phones with Shizuku.
- **Read translation activity on hover.** The desktop subtitle window shows “Translating” while a streaming service returns unfinished text. Hover to see the activity and latest timing beside the tools, or below them in a narrow window. Open the language panel for separate API and translation timings.
- The compact desktop controls show the full **Paused** and **Unlock** labels. The paused language pair is available on hover and in the panel. If resizing the window closes a language search, keyboard focus returns to the field. Dragging the scrollbar back to the bottom resumes live captions even if the last scroll arrives after you let go.
- Settings and the floating panel now show the result of refreshing the audio app list without moving the controls. Opening the list during a refresh preserves both its result and keyboard focus.

Thanks to @Excarius for suggesting the Android shortcuts, explaining how screen locking interrupted their captions, and testing Gemini Live with a video longer than ten minutes. [Issue #401](https://github.com/yuxino/Mimi/issues/401)

## 中文

- **在 Android 上一边看视频，一边启停字幕。** 打开「更多 → 字幕快捷入口」，把「Mimi 字幕」添加到系统快捷设置。还可以分别打开屏边悬浮入口和字幕停止时的通知，两项都有独立开关，字幕运行期间也能关闭。字幕停止时可以从通知开始，运行时可以从通知停止。Android 13 及以上的新安装，首次从 Mimi 内开始字幕时可能会询问一次通知权限。使用快捷入口启动会跳过这条可选提示。
- 把 Mimi 拖到屏幕左侧或右侧。轻点头像，再点旁边的「开始」或「停止」即可启停字幕。文字跟随系统字号，按住时会一直显示。三秒没有操作，文字收起，Mimi 留在屏边。停止字幕也会结束音频采集。
- 如果锁屏中断了**仅使用系统声音**的字幕，允许系统通知后，解锁时可点「继续字幕」，确认 Android 的共享提示就能回到视频。关闭常驻通知后仍可收到这条提醒。使用了麦克风的会话会保持停止。本版每次重新采集系统声音都使用 Android 标准授权流程，装有 Shizuku 的手机也一样。
- **在桌面字幕窗查看翻译进度。** 流式服务陆续返回译文时，鼠标移入字幕窗即可看到「翻译中」和最近一次耗时。两者显示在工具旁，窄窗口会放到工具下方。展开语言面板可分别查看接口和翻译耗时。
- 桌面工具栏能完整显示暂停状态和解锁按钮。暂停时可将鼠标移到语言栏上，或展开面板，查看原文与译文语言。调整窗口大小导致语言搜索收起时，键盘焦点会回到原字段。回看字幕后快速把滚动条拖到底，即使松手后才收到最后一次滚动，也会继续跟随实时字幕。
- 在设置或悬浮面板刷新音频应用列表，现在会提示刷新结果，控件位置保持稳定。刷新过程中打开列表，仍能看到刷新结果，也能继续用键盘搜索。

感谢 @Excarius 提出 Android 字幕快捷入口，说明锁屏中断的使用场景，并用超过十分钟的视频验证了 Gemini Live 能继续运行。[议题 #401](https://github.com/yuxino/Mimi/issues/401)
