## English

- Fixed batch deletion and "Clear all" for saved desktop sessions (#427, reported by @yeshuoer): added `session_history_delete_many` to the settings-window Tauri permission list with compile-time ACL contract tests, surfaced sanitized localized deletion failure reasons across all 9 UI languages, and fixed a bug where deleting a previously viewed session cleared the completion or partial-failure toast.
- Fixed the missing target language picker in the active Apple Translation service editor, restoring target language selection before resource readiness checks without binding inactive configurations.
- Split oversized frontend, Rust, and Android modules and stabilized CI checks without changing user-facing runtime behavior.

Update from Mimi or download the installer for your platform. This release also includes the signed Android APK. Validation covers full automated desktop, shared-runtime, real JNI, and Android regressions, plus local signed macOS development-app verification for batch history deletion; physical Android devices and full multi-provider live sessions were not retested for this release.

## 中文

- 修复桌面端批量删除与“全部清空”已保存记录失败的问题（#427，感谢 @yeshuoer 反馈）：在设置窗口的 Tauri 权限清单中补齐 `session_history_delete_many` 并新增编译期 ACL 契约测试，在 9 种界面语言中展示脱敏后的具体删除失败原因，并修复删除曾浏览记录时误清空完成或部分失败提示的问题。
- 修复当前生效的 Apple 翻译配置编辑器中缺少目标语言选择器的问题，恢复在资源就绪检查前选择目标语言，且不误绑定未启用配置。
- 拆分部分超大前端、Rust 与 Android 模块并稳定 CI 检查（不改变面向用户的运行时行为）。

可在 Mimi 内更新，或下载对应平台的安装包；本版同步提供签名的 Android 安装包。验证覆盖桌面端、共享层、真实 JNI 与 Android 自动回归，以及签名 macOS 开发包上的批量删除保存记录实测；未针对本版复测 Android 真机或全供应商实时长会话。
