## English

- Added a quick vertical background-opacity slider directly on the left edge of the floating subtitle overlay across Desktop and Android (#432, suggested by @Excarius): reveals on hover on Desktop (including nonactivating windows, vertical drag, scroll-wheel `±5%`, and keyboard control) and lives cleanly inside the left margin on Android with live 60fps updates, automatically hiding whenever the overlay is locked or in Immersive Mode.
- Added customizable overlay background colors (defaulting to pure black `#000000` at 80% opacity) plus direct `#RRGGBB` HEX input and color pickers for both subtitle text and background colors across Desktop and Android (native HSV + HEX picker on Android), localized across all 9 UI languages.
- Added automatic contrast adaptation for light opaque overlay surfaces (dark neutral chrome, status island, latency, timestamps, dividers, and subtle light-card outline) and adaptive legibility shadows when background opacity drops below 35% (down to `0%`), plus Windows High Contrast (`forced-colors`) support.

Update from Mimi or download the installer for your platform. This release also includes the signed Android APK. Validation covers full automated desktop, shared-runtime, real JNI, and Android release/debug regressions, plus Android 15 emulator interaction checks (light/dark mode, landscape picker, and native overlay controls); physical Android devices and full multi-provider live sessions were not retested for this release.

## 中文

- 在桌面端与 Android 浮窗左侧新增快捷背景不透明度滑块（#432，感谢 @Excarius 建议）：桌面端悬停显现（支持非激活浮窗悬停、竖向拖动、滚轮 `±5%` 与键盘步进），Android 端置于左侧留白内支持 60fps 实时调节，锁定浮窗位置或进入沉浸模式时自动隐藏。
- 双端新增自定义浮窗背景颜色（保持纯黑 `#000000` 与 80% 不透明度为默认值），并为字幕文字与背景颜色补齐 `#RRGGBB` HEX 输入及取色器（Android 端新增原生 HSV + HEX 双向同步调色弹窗），完整覆盖全部 9 种界面语言。
- 浅色不透明背景自动切换状态岛、工具栏、延迟栏、时间戳、分隔线与卡片细描边为深色中性墨色；背景不透明度低于 35%（含 `0%`）时自动启用按字色明暗区分的对比阴影，并补齐桌面端折叠态与 Windows 高对比度模式（`forced-colors`）适配。

可在 Mimi 内更新，或下载对应平台的安装包；本版同步提供签名的 Android 安装包。验证覆盖桌面端、共享层、真实 JNI 与 Android Release/Debug 自动回归，以及 Android 15 模拟器上的浅/深色模式、横屏调色弹窗与原生浮窗交互实测；未针对本版复测 Android 真机或全供应商实时长会话。
