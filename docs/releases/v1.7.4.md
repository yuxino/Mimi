## English

- Refined the floating subtitle controls, with quicker access to language, audio and display options. Subtitle display and position-lock shortcuts are now visible in Settings.
- Kept keyboard focus when saving language choices or reconnecting recognition.
- Restored live-caption following after dragging the scrollbar back to the bottom.
- Improved recovery from OpenAI Realtime session expiry and clarified permission, quota and rate-limit errors.
- Improved subtitle responsiveness on Linux X11 with software rendering, and reduced Linux capture-stop waits.

Update in the app or install the package for your platform. An Android update is included.

Validation covers automated regressions and isolated native UI/package checks. Quality and long sessions with live services, global shortcuts with a physical keyboard, and physical Android devices were not retested for this release.

## 中文

- 优化悬浮字幕控件，更方便地调整语言、音频和显示选项；设置中直接显示字幕显示和位置锁定的快捷键。
- 保存语言选择或识别重连后，仍可继续用键盘操作。
- 将滚动条拖回底部后，恢复跟随实时字幕。
- 改善 OpenAI Realtime 会话到期后的恢复，并更清楚地说明权限、额度和限流错误。
- 改善 Linux X11 软件渲染时的字幕响应，减少 Linux 停止采音时的等待。

可在应用内更新，或安装对应平台的安装包。本版包含 Android 更新。

验证覆盖自动回归及隔离环境中的原生界面／安装包检查；本次未复测真实服务的字幕质量与长会话、物理键盘全局快捷键或 Android 真机表现。
