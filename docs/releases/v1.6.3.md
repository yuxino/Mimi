## English

- Prevented late drafts from overwriting the current translation and fixed translation waiting states that could remain stuck on desktop.
- Improved playback and export recovery when recording finalization fails.
- Reduced unnecessary activity after leaving the saved-session page and prevented repeated playback clicks from loading the same recording multiple times.
- Fixed Apple Translation language-pack status after switching languages or returning to settings.

Update from Settings → Software Update, or download the installer below. Portable Windows users can replace their existing executable after quitting Mimi.

Contributor: @yuxino.

## 中文

- 防止迟到的草稿覆盖当前译文，修复桌面版可能一直停留在等待翻译状态的问题。
- 改善录音收尾失败后的播放和导出恢复。
- 减少离开保存记录页面后的后台读取，防止重复点击播放时多次加载同一份录音。
- 修复切换语言或返回设置页后 Apple 翻译语言包的状态显示。

可在「设置 → 软件更新」中更新，也可下载下方安装包。Windows 便携版请先退出 Mimi，再替换原有程序。

贡献者：@yuxino。
