## English

Fixed an Android subtitle issue during short pauses. A translated line could disappear too soon and the overlay would show “Listening”. It now keeps the current translation visible. If a preview is withdrawn, the overlay can show the last confirmed caption for that audio source.

Reproduced on an Android 15 emulator and retested after the fix. To try it on your device, install the new APK over your existing version.

Thanks to @xuanli20 for reporting #336 and @yuxino for the fix.

## 中文

修复安卓端说话短暂停顿时，译文过早消失、浮窗变回“正在聆听”的问题。现在停顿时会保留当前译文。如果服务撤回了预览，浮窗可以显示同一音源上一条已确认的字幕。

已在 Android 15 模拟器上复现并验证修复。安卓用户可下载新版 APK 覆盖安装后重试。

感谢 @xuanli20 在 #336 反馈，感谢 @yuxino 修复。
