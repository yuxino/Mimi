## Baidu failure and content-free trace

The A trace confirms system input, English → Chinese, bilingual display and
recorded content off. Capture started at trace-relative 9.335 s; an accepted
provider error arrived at 63.504 s, after **54.169 s**. The trace records only
`error` and `provider_terminal_error`, with no specific Baidu error code or
numeric status. Its server-side cause cannot be determined from this trace.

- Six final-pair events were accepted; retained history grew from one to seven
  groups. The native observation, separately, confirmed the sample's final
  sentence. Metadata alone cannot establish translation correctness.
- The last send-statistic observation was at 63.345 s, just 159 ms before the
  error: 2,700 cumulative buffers / 1,728,000 bytes at -96 dBFS. The last
  non-silent observation was at 33.351 s; 15 subsequent -96 dBFS observations
  span 35.345–63.345 s. Maximum send-statistic observation gap was 2,007 ms,
  **not a network-frame interval**. This does not support attributing the error
  to 30 seconds without sent audio.
- All 1,351 trace entries were retained without eviction, drops or write
  failure. Content, private-event and replay counters were zero. All 77
  snapshots were delivered, received and applied by settings, overlay,
  overlay-control and tray-panel, with zero source/translation/history/track
  count mismatches. Overlay commits covered all 77 snapshots; first-commit
  delay had a 53 ms median and 63 ms maximum. Trace stop left no unflushed
  windows. This is the batch's detailed multiwindow evidence, not a claim that
  every other provider received equivalent tracing.

Baidu can still fail during silence despite continued sending. Earlier recovery
and new successful starts do not establish sustained recovery or explain the
termination. No speculative server timeout or new automatic-restart behavior
is claimed.
